# Ink — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 24, 2026_

Ink is a life-calendar journal. This policy explains what we collect and why.
Host this page publicly and link it from the App Store listing and in-app.

## What we collect

- **Account**: your email address and a hashed password, managed by Firebase
  Authentication (Google LLC), used only to sign you in.
- **Your journal**: week entries (sentence, rating, goal names, settings) are stored
  on your device and backed up to your account (Google Cloud Firestore) so they can
  be restored when you sign in on a new device.
- **Photos**: photos you attach to a week stay **on your device**. They are not
  uploaded to our servers.
- **Purchases**: subscription status is processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We never
  see your payment details.

## What we don't do

- No ads. No analytics trackers. No selling or sharing of personal data.
- No access to your photo library beyond the photos you explicitly pick.

## Notifications

Check-in reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They never leave it.

## Data retention & deletion

Delete your account any time in **Account → Delete Account**. This permanently
removes your account and everything backed up to it. Data on your device stays
until you delete the app.

## Children

Ink is not directed at children under 13.
